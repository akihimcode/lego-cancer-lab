About Me

Mihika Prabhu

I created Rebuilding Life to make cancer biology more accessible through creativity and technology. By combining biomedical research, STEM education, and LEGO-based visualization, this project aims to help students understand the complex processes behind cancer. At the age of 12, my cousin Shlok, passed away with rhabdomyosarcoma. At his time in the hospital, he build over 10+ LEGO creations. This is for you, Shlok. I love and miss you endlessly.
