Rebuilding Life:
Cancer Through LEGO

An interactive journey through biology, where complex cancer concepts are rebuilt one LEGO brick at a time.

Explore the Models

Why LEGO?

Cancer biology is often invisible. Through LEGO models, this project transforms cells, organs, mutations, and treatments into something people can see, build, and understand.
